Emergency Faucet Repair in Etna, OH

Faucet repair is the fixing of the valve, cartridge, seals and supply connections behind a tap. A person answers 24/7 when one will not shut off.

A faucet or valve that will not shut off, first things first

Before anything else, find the closest shutoff and turn it clockwise. Which one depends on where the water is coming from.

Spray from the faucet body

Close the two angle stops under the sink. They are the small oval or round handles on the supply lines, and a quarter turn is often enough.

Stops that will not turn

If an angle stop spins freely or is frozen with mineral deposits, go to the main water valve, usually where the line enters the house. Do not force a seized stop.

Shower or tub valve running

There is rarely a stop behind the wall. Shut off the main valve and open a lower faucet to drain pressure while you wait.

Water on the ceiling below

Close the main, then call. A person answers 24/7, and a leaking wall valve is not a wait-until-morning problem.

Cartridge, washer or ball: what is inside your faucet

Almost every faucet drips for a reason that can be named. Compression faucets use a rubber washer that hardens and splits, while single-handle faucets rely on a cartridge or a ceramic disc that wears or clogs with grit.

That difference decides the repair. A washer takes minutes to swap, a cartridge takes a matching part, and a worn ball-type faucet may need the seats and springs replaced as well.

Matching a replacement cartridge to an older faucet takes time when there is no brand name on it. We bring the common sizes and tell you if a part must be ordered.

Leak under the sink: tracing it before we replace anything

A wet cabinet floor does not always mean the faucet. Water follows the lowest path, and the actual source can sit a foot away from the puddle.

  1. 1

    Dry everything

    Wipe the supply lines, the faucet shanks and the drain trap with a paper towel. A dry towel is the best leak detector in the house.

  2. 2

    Run the water and watch

    Turn on the cold, then the hot, with a flashlight and a fresh towel under each connection. Note which line drips and when.

  3. 3

    Fill the sink and release

    A leak that shows only when the basin drains comes from the trap, the strainer or the drain tailpiece. That is a drain fitting, not the faucet.

  4. 4

    Check the spout base

    Water under the faucet deck that appears only when the handle is on usually means a failed O-ring or a loose mounting nut.

Repair or replace: a quick rule

If the fixture is under ten years old and the leak is at a seal, a repair usually makes sense. If the finish is pitted, the body is corroded or parts are no longer sold, a new faucet costs less than repeated visits. We say which side of that line yours falls on before starting.

Showers, tubs and the drain that goes with them

A showerhead that sprays sideways is usually mineral buildup in the nozzles. Soaking it in white vinegar for an hour often brings it back. A shower valve that drips for days is another story, because the cartridge behind the wall is worn and the hot side may be passing through unseen.

Tubs add a drain to the picture. If water pools around your ankles while the faucet runs fine, the cause is hair and soap in the trap, and our drain cleaning service clears it. If the tub faucet drips and the drain is slow at the same time, we deal with both in one visit.

Reading the water that reaches your faucets in Etna

Tap water for many township homes is supplied by the Licking Regional Water District, which pumps groundwater at its York Road plant and nano-filters it to strip out calcium and magnesium. No hardness figure is published, so we do not guess. If a spout flow thins or white crust keeps returning on an aerator, test your tap instead of assuming the faucet is bad.

In 2025 the district's lead and copper sampling stayed below the action levels, and it reports no lead service lines. That says nothing about one particular house, so if your plumbing is original and you want a closer look at old fittings, we can talk it through. Homes on a private well can differ from the public supply, and the county health department regulates wells. Neighbors in Pataskala draw from the same district.
