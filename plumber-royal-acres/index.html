Royal Acres in Etna Township

Royal Acres is a residential neighborhood along Mink Street Road in Etna Township. Census address matching places Princess Street, Imperial Drive and Roma Court inside the township, all with ZIP 43062. A national builder also markets new homes under the Royal Acres name off Mink Street.

Do not trust the mailing city. The builder lists its own Royal Acres model home with a Pataskala address, and many listing sites do the same. Pataskala is a separate city, and these streets are in the township. Permits and plumber registration therefore run through the Licking County Health Department, and the Pataskala page applies only to homes inside that city.

The creek in the township plan

The Etna Township long-range plan says a creek flows through the Royal Acres subdivision along Mink Street Road. It counts about 24 structures there as at risk in a major flood. That is a planning estimate, not a map of your lot.

To learn your own exposure, look up your address on the county floodplain page, which carries the FEMA flood maps. Then test your pump on a dry day by pouring a bucket of water into the pit and watching the float rise. Our sump pump and basement waterproofing pages explain the next step.

Water and sewer service

The township plan says central water and sewer lines let it retire small neighborhood treatment plants, and it names Royal Acres as one of them. The plan text carries no date we could confirm, so check that the arrangement still holds for your address.

The Licking Regional Water District, at 740-927-0410, can tell you who supplies water and sewer to your street. Whatever its answer, the line inside your property is yours. We handle that side, including a camera inspection when something does not add up.

Before the visit

A few quick checks help us arrive prepared.

  1. 1

    Find the pit

    Note whether the sump pit has a lid and whether any water sits in it. Do not put a hand in while the pump has power.

  2. 2

    Check the outlet

    Make sure the pump plug sits in a working outlet and that the discharge pipe outside is not blocked or frozen.

  3. 3

    Move belongings off the floor

    Lift boxes and electronics. Keep people away from standing water that is near outlets or the panel.

  4. 4

    Tell us what you saw

    Say when the water appeared, whether it rained, and whether the pump was running.
